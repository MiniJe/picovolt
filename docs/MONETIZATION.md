# PicoVolt commercial model

Updated 2026-09-10. This supersedes the previous permissive-core-only revenue
thesis. Prices and services below are hypotheses, not active offers.

## What customers buy

1. A proprietary engine license for perpetual offline use of identified future
   releases, including embedding in ordinary commercial applications. Competing
   engine/platform rights require a separate agreement. The one-time price is
   undecided; a zero-fee developer grant is an option, not an announced offer.
2. A PicoVolt Hub subscription for private dataset storage, versions, delivery,
   team access and rollback. Meter storage, transfer and retained releases with
   explicit quotas/overage choices. Lifetime engine rights exclude lifetime hosting.
3. Support and design partnerships for integration, migration and performance
   work. Require the applicable license and written support scope; acceptance
   alone promises no SLA.
4. Later enterprise agreements for LTS, private deployment, OEM redistribution
   or competing-platform rights, negotiated explicitly.

Versions through 2.0.0 remain Apache-2.0. Voluntary legacy maintenance ends on
2026-09-10, subject to existing commitments and mandatory obligations. A 30-day
migration assistance window starts when a usable successor and guide exist;
there is no expiry or account requirement for old copies. See
[the transition](../legal/TRANSITION.md) and [support](SUPPORT.md).

## Validate before expansion

Start with one team distributing catalogs or offline reference data. Obtain a
representative dataset and agree a paid manual release workflow before building
billing or a broad platform. Measure engineering hours, storage, outbound bytes,
retention and support. Automate repeated steps from [the Hub roadmap](HUB_ROADMAP.md).

Internal interview anchors: USD 19/month for an individual Hub project and
USD 79/month for a team. Limits and infrastructure costs are unmeasured. These
are willingness-to-pay questions, not website prices or checkout offers.

First success gate: one paying pilot completes two releases and a rollback,
with positive contribution margin after infrastructure and support. Expand on
repeat use and renewal. If customers only value support, sell support.

Measure activated projects, repeat releases, recovery time, cost and renewals.
Registry downloads are not customer counts or revenue forecasts. Accounts may
record disclosed delivery and entitlement events; no covert local monitoring,
SQL/data collection or competitor profiling belongs in the product.
