2026. `PUBLIC-RELEASE.json` and root `LICENSE` identify the zero-fee grant under

and vault entry points in native and language interfaces, and their new tests
and documentation introduced after the public 2.0.0 release. This covers only
rights controlled by the Licensor. Historical Apache-2.0 code and the full-text
implementation retain their existing notices; dependency licenses remain intact.

Binary distributions include dependency notices and source references. The
historical 2.1 private scope ledger remains a record of the earlier plan.
